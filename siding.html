Choose your material

Four ways to side your home

Most popular

Vinyl

An affordable, low-maintenance option available in a wide range of colours and profiles. A popular choice for homeowners looking for lasting curb appeal without ongoing upkeep.

Best for:Budget-friendly renovations, new homes & rental properties

Aluminum

Lightweight, durable, and resistant to rot, insects, and corrosion. An excellent choice for coastal environments where moisture and salt are concerns.

Best for:Coastal homes & commercial buildings

Steel

Our most durable siding option. Built to withstand high winds, hail, and impact while providing exceptional fire resistance and a clean, modern appearance with minimal maintenance.

Best for:Maximum durability & contemporary designs

Engineered Wood

The natural beauty of real wood with the added durability of modern engineering. Designed to stand up to the Maritime climate while requiring far less maintenance than traditional wood siding.

Best for: Premium curb appeal with lower long-term maintenance
