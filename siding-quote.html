Four ways to side your home

Most popular

Vinyl

An affordable, low-maintenance option available in a wide range of colours and profiles. A popular choice for lasting curb appeal without ongoing upkeep.

Best for: Budget-friendly renovations, new homes & rental properties

Aluminum

Lightweight, durable, and resistant to rot, insects, and corrosion. An excellent choice for coastal environments where moisture and salt are concerns.

Best for: Coastal homes & commercial buildings

Steel

Our most durable siding option. Built to withstand high winds, hail, and impact while providing exceptional fire resistance and a clean, modern look.

Best for: Maximum durability & contemporary designs

Engineered Wood

The natural beauty of real wood with the added durability of modern engineering. Stands up to the Maritime climate with far less maintenance than traditional wood.

Best for: Premium curb appeal with lower long-term maintenance

The siding brands we stock

We’re a dealer for the brands below, and we sell them direct at supplier pricing. Ask us to price any product from any of these lines.

Kaycan

Canadian-made vinyl and aluminum siding, soffit, fascia and trim. One of the widest colour ranges we stock, and the go-to for most Moncton-area re-sides.

Maibec

Prefinished wood and engineered wood siding with factory-applied finishes built for Canadian weather. A premium look with a warranty behind the coating.

LP SmartSide

Engineered wood lap, panel and trim. The real-wood look with far better impact and moisture performance than traditional wood siding.

NewTechWood

Composite cladding and decking in a capped, colour-stable finish — a modern option for feature walls and full exteriors.

Canyon Stone

Manufactured stone veneer to pair with your siding — skirting, columns, feature walls and full facades.
