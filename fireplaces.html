Choose your fireplace

Electric or propane, from Napoleon

Electric

No venting, no gas line and no chimney, so an electric fireplace can go on almost any wall. Realistic flames with or without heat, all year round.

Best for:Basements, condos, bedrooms & blank walls

Propane

A real flame and real heat at the push of a button, with none of the work of wood. Keeps you warm when the power goes out in a Maritime storm.

Best for:Living rooms, heating a space & cottages

Fireplace questions, answered

A package brings together the three things a fireplace wall needs: the fireplace unit, the stone that surrounds it, and a mantel. You pick each one with our help, and we quote it as one package so you're not pricing three separate jobs.

We're an authorized Napoleon dealer. Napoleon is a Canadian manufacturer with a long track record and solid warranties. For the surround, you can choose from the stone lines we stock, including Eldorado Stone, Mathios Stone, Canyon Stone Canada and Colonial Brick & Stone.

Electric fireplaces need no venting or gas line, so they can go almost anywhere, including a blank wall in a basement or condo. Propane gives you a real flame and real heat, and keeps working during a power outage, but needs venting and a gas line. We'll walk you through which one suits your space.
